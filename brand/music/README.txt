Music for Instagram Reels, matched to the film's country.

Put royalty-free instrumental tracks (mp3/m4a/wav) in the folder for each region:
  italy/        Italy
  nordic/       Denmark, Iceland, Sweden, Norway, Finland
  greece/       Greece, Cyprus
  middle-east/  Israel, Iran, Palestine, Lebanon, Turkey, Tunisia, Morocco
  latin/        Spain, Argentina, Brazil, Mexico, Chile, Panama
  france/       France, Belgium, Netherlands
  europe/       Germany, the Balkans, Central & Eastern Europe, UK (any other country)
  asia/         Japan, Korea, Taiwan, Philippines, India
  general/      used when a region folder is empty

Each Reel picks one track from its region (the same film always gets the same track),
with a fade in and out. Only use tracks whose licence allows use on social media,
and avoid tracks marked "Content ID" on Pixabay (they can trigger copyright claims).
