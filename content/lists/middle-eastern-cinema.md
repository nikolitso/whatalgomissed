A baker who falls in love and ends up working as an informant. A surgeon whose wife turns out to be a stranger. A father crossing a wall illegally to reach his hospitalised son 200 meters away. Women in a Beirut beauty salon, a lemon grove on the wrong side of a fence, a karaoke night in a Tel Aviv high-rise. **The politics are everywhere — but the films are about people.**
