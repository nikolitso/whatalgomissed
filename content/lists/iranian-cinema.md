Iran has been making masterpieces from inside a cage for decades — and that constraint has produced some of the most **morally precise, emotionally devastating** filmmaking in the world. These aren't films about politics. They're films about people: a couple unraveling in a courtroom, a doctor who can't look a grieving father in the eye, a man who refuses to pay a bribe. From Farhadi to Rasoulof — ordinary people placed under extraordinary pressure, and the impossible choices that follow.
