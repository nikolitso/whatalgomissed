Greek cinema at its most unsparing. Raw, claustrophobic, politically honest — a cinema built entirely in the shadow of crisis. Yannis Economides cornering you in an Athens apartment until you can't breathe. Tsangari watching men compete over nothing on a boat in the Aegean. Films that show exactly how things got this bad, and make you sit with it.
