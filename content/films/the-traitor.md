The true story of Tommaso Buscetta — the first major Mafia boss to break omertà and testify against Cosa Nostra, causing its leadership to collapse. Bellocchio directs with operatic intensity and Pierfrancesco Favino disappears entirely into the role, making a man whose choices are completely morally ambiguous completely comprehensible. Six David di Donatello Awards including Best Film.
