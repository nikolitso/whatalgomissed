Christoffer has built a life in Sweden with his Swedish partner — far from his family's industrial dynasty in Denmark. Then his father commits suicide and his mother summons him back to take over the steelworks. Per Fly's second part of the trilogy moves from the working class to the upper class with the same unflinching eye. About what duty costs and how much of yourself disappears in the process of becoming what others need you to be. Quiet, devastating, and impeccably performed.
