A middle-aged man who has lived his entire life in the closet — married, respected, deeply buried — finds himself at a crossroads between the comfortable lie he has built over decades and something that finally resembles truth. A film about the specific cost of self-concealment across a lifetime: the energy spent maintaining the performance, the people damaged by the distance, the self slowly disappeared.
