A retired Georgian schoolteacher travels to Istanbul to find her long-lost transgender niece. What begins as a search film — procedural, purposeful — becomes something quieter and more profound: about the borders we cross between who we were and who we're willing to become. Levan Akin works with the same warmth and precision as his earlier And Then We Danced. Won the Teddy Award at Berlin 2024.
