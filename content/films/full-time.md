A single mother raising two children in the Paris suburbs tries to get to a job interview in the city during a transit strike. Gravel shoots it like a thriller — relentless, claustrophobic, scored with pounding electronica — about how just staying financially afloat can feel like a white-knuckle survival exercise. Laure Calamy won Best Actress at Venice for a performance of sustained, exhausting credibility.
