A Copenhagen crime scene photographer is involved in a minor car accident. At the hospital, the seriously injured young woman's family mistakes him for her boyfriend — and he doesn't correct them. Bornedal's neo-noir starts as a dark romantic comedy and turns into something considerably darker. Slick, twisty, and confident — the kind of well-constructed genre thriller Danish cinema occasionally produces and rarely exports.
