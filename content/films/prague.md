A married Danish couple travel to Prague to collect the body of the husband's estranged father. The father's phone rings — a woman's voice, a life Christoffer knew nothing about. What follows is a quiet masterpiece about grief, estrangement, and the things we never got to say to the people we decided not to know. One of the most underrated Danish films of the decade, almost entirely unknown outside Denmark.
