Washed-up musician Yiannis is finally leaving crisis-ridden Cyprus for a better life abroad — until his dog, Jimi, escapes across the UN buffer zone into the Turkish-occupied north. To get him back, Yiannis must navigate a bureaucratic and political absurdity that the island has lived with for forty years. Piperides uses a dog and a divided city to say something genuinely honest about borders, belonging, and a conflict that has become so normalized that even its absurdity no longer shocks. Funny, warm, and quietly furious. The best Cypriot film I have seen.
