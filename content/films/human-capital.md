A hit-and-run accident the night before Christmas connects three families across class lines in Lombardy. Told from multiple perspectives — each one revealing something the others concealed. A sharp, well-constructed thriller with class complicity and financial speculation at its centre. Virzì's most formally controlled film. Won the David di Donatello for Best Film.
