A 50-year-old married teacher in a well-ordered middle-class life is having an affair with a younger political activist. When she is involved in a killing, his carefully structured existence begins to collapse. The third and final part of Per Fly's class trilogy completes his portrait of Danish society from the bottom (The Bench) through the middle (Manslaughter) to the top (The Inheritance) — each film showing how class shapes not just circumstance but character. Won the Nordic Council Film Prize.
