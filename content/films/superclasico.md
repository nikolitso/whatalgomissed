Danish wine seller Christian receives divorce papers from his wife — who has now moved to Buenos Aires and is dating an Argentinian football star. He flies to Buenos Aires to win her back. The warmest Danish film on this list by a considerable margin — a fish-out-of-water comedy that actually earns its warmth. Danish cinema occasionally allows itself to be charming, and when it does, the results are genuinely lovely. Denmark's Oscar submission that year.
