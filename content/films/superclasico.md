Christian is a Danish wine seller whose shop, and life, are going nowhere. Then the divorce papers arrive from his wife Anna, who has moved to Buenos Aires, works as a football agent and is now dating one of her clients, an Argentinian football star. Christian flies out with their teenage son to sign the papers. Secretly, he plans to win her back.

Anders W. Berthelsen plays Christian as a hopeless, likeable man who has let life drift past him, and Paprika Steen is sharp and funny as the wife who has clearly moved on. Ole Christian Madsen, better known for the war drama Flame & Citron and the bleak Prague, lets the sun in for once. Buenos Aires is full of colour, tango and football, and the film enjoys it all without turning it into a postcard.

The warmest Danish film on this list by a considerable margin: a fish-out-of-water comedy that actually earns its warmth. It is not trying to change your life, and it doesn't need to. Danish cinema occasionally allows itself to be charming, and when it does, the results are genuinely lovely. It was Denmark's Oscar submission that year. A perfect film for an evening with a good bottle of red, preferably Argentinian.
