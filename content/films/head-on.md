Desperate to escape her suffocatingly traditional Turkish-German family, young Sibel convinces disillusioned alcoholic Cahit to enter a marriage of convenience. Two lost souls who gradually, against every instinct, fall in love — and then destroy what they've found. Raw, chaotic, and deeply felt. Won the Golden Bear at Berlin 2004. Fatih Akin's most visceral film.
