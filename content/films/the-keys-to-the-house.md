A father meets his severely disabled teenage son for the first time. Amelio's film refuses sentiment at every turn — the relationship that develops is uncomfortable, clumsy, and ultimately more generous than most films that try to be kind. The boy Paolo is played by Andrea Rossi with remarkable openness. A film that trusts you to bring your own feelings to it rather than telling you what to feel.
