Seven long-time friends gather for dinner and agree to put every phone call on speaker and share every message they receive during the evening. Secrets begin to surface. A deceptively simple premise that becomes a razor-sharp dissection of modern intimacy, trust, and the things we hide from the people we're supposed to be closest to. Holds the Guinness World Record as the most remade film in history.
