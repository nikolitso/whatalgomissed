A construction worker loses his wife in childbirth and tries to hold everything together — his three children, his grief, his sense of himself as a man — by throwing himself into dangerous work and worse decisions. Elio Germano won Best Actor at Cannes for a performance of sustained physical and emotional rawness. The most Italian film about masculinity and grief I have seen.
