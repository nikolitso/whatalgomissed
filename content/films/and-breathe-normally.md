A struggling Icelandic single mother working as a border guard flags the passport of an asylum seeker from Guinea-Bissau. What begins as a bureaucratic act becomes something more human and more complicated than either woman planned. About two people trapped by systems — one by poverty, one by bureaucracy — and the unexpected solidarity that forms between them in the space those systems leave. Won the Directing Award at Sundance.
