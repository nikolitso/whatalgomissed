Some films take years to find you. This is where they can.

This is a personal map of world cinema — built from the conviction that the most honest, most alive filmmaking often comes from far beyond Hollywood — the places the algorithm tends to miss. From Greek apartments sweltering in August heat. From Danish families sitting down to dinners that will take decades to recover from. From Iranian directors making films under impossible pressure. From Palestinian men crossing walls to reach their children.

Every list here began as a private obsession. The write-ups are my own. The ratings come from my [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/nikolitso/). Every film is here because it stayed with me.

If you discover something here that stays with you — that's what this is for.

*— Antonis Nikolitsopoulos, Thessaloniki, Greece*
