Some films take years to find you. This is where they can.

This is a personal map of world cinema, built on a simple belief: the most honest, most alive filmmaking often happens far beyond Hollywood, in the places the algorithm tends to miss. Greek apartments sweltering in the August heat. Danish families sitting down to dinners that will take decades to recover. Iranian directors making films under impossible pressure. Palestinian men crossing walls to reach their children.

Every list began as a private obsession. The write-ups are my own, and the ratings are the ones I give on [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/nikolitso/). Each film earned its place by staying with me.

If you discover something here that stays with you too, that's what this is for.

*— Antonis Nikolitsopoulos, Greece*
